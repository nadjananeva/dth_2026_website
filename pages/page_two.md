# Open source part 2

## The cons

Just as open source has its benefits, it also has its downsides which are equally important to consider before using. Here is another top 5 that might clarify the cons of open source:

<ol>
<li>Lack of support
Open source projects are reliant on a community-driven support system rather that the traditional customer support idea, which can disadvantage the need for quick help (Muñoz, 2025) </li>
<li> Security risk
Given open sources popularity, one of the downsides is that one potential entry point can cause huge damage into thousands of systems at once if something goes wrong (Team, 2026; Mayo, 2024) </li>
<li>Quality and maintenance
A majority of open source projects are carried out by single contributors, so it is only possible if people stay engaged as abandoned projects carry risks (Ulac, 2025; Schweigert, 2024)</li>
<li>Copyright laws
Restrictive licenses can create intellectual property risk, and if a user handles a licence improperly it can lead to legal complications (SentinelOne, 2025)<li>
<li> Knowledge or lack thereof
In some cases there might a requirement for in-house expertise which can cause a demand for a greater internal engineering capability and software maintenance compared to vendor-supported technologies (Pratt, 2022)</li>
</ol>
