# Open source

## The pros

<p> There are many reasons behind using open source tools. One of the major reasons many companies join are the financial benefits, such as reduced costs (Cemazar, 2024). To show how else open source is useful, here is a top 5: </p>

<ol> <li>Transparency </li>
There can be many people reviewing, leading to more mistakes being caught. For a user this mutual growth can be helpful and builds a trust between them and the provider (Muñoz, 2025).
<li>No vendor lock in </li>
In some cases a business might go bankrupt or a vendor who stops maintenance. With open source you yourself can modify the software into what your particular needs are, which is beneficial for long-term stability in projects (Muñoz, 2025)
<li>Flexibility and customisation</li>
This is similar to the point above, but here innovation occurs based on inspiration from a user and not need (Mitton, 2024) 
<li>Communication and innovation</li>
Open source creates a feeling of community and drives through collaboration innovative ideas forward as 97% of applications today are built with open source (Achille, 2026)
<li>Implementation</li>
 In business it is often seen that teams start with free options and with their continuous growth they move on to commercially supported versions (Muñoz, 2025).

</ol>
